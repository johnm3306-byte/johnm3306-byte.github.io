01 / About

Between the launch pad and the press room.

I'm a Nairobi-based communications professional, currently Marketing and Communications Specialist at the Research Institute for Innovation and Sustainability (RIIS). Before that I told the stories of Digital Earth Africa, the Kenya Space Agency and Leo Sky Africa.

My background is in journalism, with training in African and global space policy. I care most about space sustainability, global space governance and bringing the public into Africa's emerging space ecosystems.
