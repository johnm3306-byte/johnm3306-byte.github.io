About

Between the newsroom and the night sky.

I'm a Marketing and Communications Specialist at the Research Institute for Innovation and Sustainability (RIIS). Before that I supported communications at Digital Earth Africa, the Kenya Space Agency and Leo Sky Africa.

My background is in journalism, with training in African and global space policy. I care most about space sustainability, global space governance and helping the public see, understand and take part in Africa's growing space sector. Away from work I'm an amateur astronomer, run an outdoor adventure brand, @thetryingathlete, and write about life on the trail on Medium.
